North Quay Institute students submit attendance appeals through the student records portal.
Attendance appeal decisions are sent to the student's institutional email address.
The registrar issues an attendance appeal decision within ten working days of receiving a complete appeal.
Students register for reassessment through the assessment portal.
A reassessment registration requires the module code and the assessment component.
The assessment office publishes reassessment room allocations two working days before the reassessment period.
Reassessment marks for taught modules are capped at the pass threshold.
