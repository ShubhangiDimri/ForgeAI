Quay Marine standard lead time is 5 business days after purchase order acknowledgment.
Quay Marine ships domestic orders on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
Quay Marine accepts unopened standard parts for return within 30 calendar days of delivery.
Quay Marine charges a 10 percent restocking fee on approved returns.
Quay Marine requires a return authorization number before any return shipment.
Quay Marine does not accept returns on custom-machined parts.
