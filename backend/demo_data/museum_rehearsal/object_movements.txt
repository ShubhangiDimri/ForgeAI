M1. Book internal movements two working days in advance. The booking includes the object identifier, destination, route, time, and names of both handlers.
M2. Each internal move requires two trained handlers. For freestanding objects heavier than 20 kilograms, a padded trolley is also mandatory.
M3. Close the gallery to visitors during a move across a public floor. Reopen it after the lead handler confirms the path is clear.
M4. Outgoing transit uses a rigid crate and an external shock indicator. Record the indicator seal number before dispatch.
M5. The receiving handler verifies the object identifier against the movement log and signs at destination. The registrar updates the location once both signatures are recorded.
