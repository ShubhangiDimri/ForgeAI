E1. Store paper objects at 45 to 55 percent relative humidity. Store metal objects at 35 to 45 percent. The wood store has a separate 45 to 60 percent band.
E2. A reading out of range for 30 consecutive minutes requires a conservation ticket. Include the space, latest reading, start time, and object materials.
E3. Paper displays have a 50-lux lighting limit. Metal displays have a 150-lux limit. Turn off case lighting when the gallery closes.
E4. Check gallery humidity sensors each Monday. Record test results in the equipment log and tell facilities if a sensor fails.
E5. Water near a collection object requires an immediate call to the duty conservator. When safe, keep visitors out of the affected space while awaiting instructions.
