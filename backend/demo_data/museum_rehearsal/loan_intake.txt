L1. A proposed incoming loan is logged 30 calendar days before its planned arrival. The registrar records the lender, object identifiers, arrival date, insurance limits, and a named contact.
L2. Complete the condition report before unpacking ends. It includes painted surfaces, mount integrity, and dated photographs. Both receiving staff sign it.
L3. Transit damage is photographed and reported to the registrar within two working hours of discovery. Do not treat or clean the object before instructions arrive.
L4. A high-value loan requires registrar and finance approval before transport booking. High value means declared value over 50,000 currency units. A standard loan needs registrar approval only.
L5. On final return, send the lender a signed receipt and archive the return report with the entry report.
