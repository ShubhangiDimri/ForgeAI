A1. An external researcher must request a study appointment ten working days in advance and provide institution, object identifiers, and purpose.
A2. A staff member must supervise the study visit. Personal bags stay in the locker. Only pencils and approved cameras can enter the study room.
A3. Personal reference photography is allowed when the registrar approves it. Publication photography requires a separate written license. Flash is prohibited.
A4. If a visitor finds damage, isolate the display and notify the duty conservator immediately. The supervisor completes an incident record before shift end.
A5. Security reviews access logs monthly. Staff may request a badge reset from their supervisor; badge issuance times are not specified.
